FAQ

Can I start training if I’ve never played tennis before?
Absolutely! Most of my students begin as complete beginners. I’ll teach you step by step — from holding the racket correctly to mastering your first rally — so you’ll feel confident from the very first session.
What do I need to bring to my first lesson?
Just comfortable sportswear, sneakers, and some water. If you don’t have your own racket yet — no worries! I’ll provide one for your first few lessons until you decide what suits you best.
What if I don’t have much free time?
No problem — we’ll build a flexible schedule that fits your routine. You can choose morning, afternoon, or evening lessons, even on weekends. Training should adapt to your life, not the other way around!
How long does it take to see real results?
It depends on how often you train, but most students start noticing visible improvements — better control, faster footwork, and stronger serves — after about 3 to 4 weeks of consistent practice.
